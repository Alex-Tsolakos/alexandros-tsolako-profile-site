  • Degree: ECE (Space), UoWM
  • Location: Kozani, Greece
  • Languages: Greek, French, English
  • Email: alexandrost2004@gmail.com

My hands-on work covers custom PCB design in Altium Designer, PID control of the rocket's airbrakes, and power distribution for all onboard avionics. Outside engineering, I've sailed for over 15 years and work as a certified Sailing and Windsurf Instructor, which taught me discipline, risk assessment and clear communication. I'm looking for internship or research opportunities in aerospace or robotics.
