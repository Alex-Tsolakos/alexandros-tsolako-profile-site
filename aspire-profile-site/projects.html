Project details

Circular Power Control Unit

A custom circular PCB power distribution board for an M-class rocket, designed from schematic and layout through fabrication and bring-up. Redundant dual-input power path management and three independent buck-converter rails power all onboard avionics.

Active Airbrake Control

Closed-loop PID control algorithms that drive servo motors to deploy the aerodynamic airbrakes during flight, developed and tuned with the avionics, software and recovery teams, with data logging for analysis.

Integration & Launch Operations

Integration, testing and flight readiness reviews for the M-class rocket, plus launch-site assembly of the solid motor, pyrotechnics and arming systems under range safety procedures.
